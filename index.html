TIPE2026

Cellular automata and cryptography

A cellular automaton is a grid of cells that all update at once, each following the same simple rule. Some rules produce patterns that look random, which is what makes them interesting for cryptography.

I have implemented a pseudorandom number generator (PRNG), a hash function, and a stream cipher in Python, all based on the Rule 30 cellular automaton. I have also conducted several tests to evaluate their performance and potential applications in cryptography.

SNU

I participated in the Service National Universel (SNU), a French national program designed to promote civic engagement, citizenship, and republican values among young people.

During my cohesion stay, I took part in various activities, including military base visits, learning military drills, and participating in flag-raising ceremonies.
